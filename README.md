## What I'm building

### QuillSail

**Creator | [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Dooba-Digital-LLC/QuillSail)**

[QuillSail](https://github.com/Dooba-Digital-LLC/QuillSail) is an open-source, self-hosted platform for blogs and newsletters where readers can contribute, not just subscribe. Built by Dooba Digital, it gives organizations control over their publications, branding, infrastructure, and data.

It brings together:

- **Publishing and contributions:** Blog and newsletter modes, Markdown editing, reader submissions, co-authors, and revision history.
- **Editorial workflows:** Review queues, feedback, change requests, approvals, and publication scheduling.
- **Community participation:** Custom member groups, permissions, writing competitions, judging, and reader voting.
- **Newsletter delivery:** Double opt-in subscriptions, audience targeting, email delivery, and reporting.
- **Self-hosting and customization:** Docker or Python deployment, themes, extensions, and configurable publication design.

QuillSail originated from work on **The Tech Stack**, a publication operated by the University of South Carolina's Association for Computing Machinery (ACM). It's now designed for other organizations to run under their own name and rules.
